Lunarc Promo Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Lunarc Promo Bot is an internal tool run by the solo developer of Lunarc, an iOS planner app. It is used only by the developer, on the developer's own Threads accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.

What the tool does

The tool publishes posts to the developer's own Threads accounts. It also searches public Threads posts for a small set of keywords about planning and weekly reflection. Search results are shown to the developer as review cards. A reply is published only after the developer reads the card and approves it by hand. Nothing is posted automatically.

Data we store

For each public post shown as a review card, the tool stores:

  • the post's ID and link (permalink)
  • the author's public username
  • the post text and its time
  • the reply the developer approved, if any, and whether it was published

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, follower lists, messages, or any other profile information. We do not like, follow, or message anyone.

How we use it

We use this data only to show the review card and to enforce limits: at most 3 replies per account per day, and at most one reply to the same author every 30 days.

The data is kept in a local database on the developer's computer. Review cards are shown in a private Discord channel that only the developer can access. The data is not shared or sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

How long we keep it

Records in the local database are deleted automatically 90 days after they are created.

Data deletion

If your public post or username appears in our records and you want it removed sooner, email jakesungjaekim@gmail.com with the link to your post or your Threads username. We will delete every matching record within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Terms of service

Lunarc Promo Bot is a private tool for its developer's own accounts, and there is no service offered to other users. Any use of Threads through the tool follows the Meta Platform Terms and the Threads Terms of Use.
